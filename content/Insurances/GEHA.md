As secondary payer, they will not cover the co-insurance on:

- Transport Chairs

> [!Tip] They will cover Medicare deductible on payable items


GEHA coordinates with Medicare for all five of its medical  
plans. GEHA waives deductibles, copays and coinsurance for  
Standard, and Elevate Plus or High plans, but not for Elevate  
or HDHP.
