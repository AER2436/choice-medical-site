## Written Order Prior to Delivery (WOPD / SWO) — Base Item

- The WOPD is an SWO received **prior to delivery**, used for the **base item only**
- May only be written **after** the face-to-face encounter is completed
- If an LCMP wheelchair specialty evaluation is also required, the SWO **cannot** be written until **both** the face-to-face AND the LCMP eval are 100% complete (performed, signed, dated, and cosigned by the treating practitioner)

### Who Must Write It
- **Only the treating practitioner** who completed the face-to-face may write the SWO for the PMD base (e.g., power wheelchair)
- No one else may fill in any content on this order

### Format Requirements
- 100% handwritten with a **wet signature and date**, OR
- 100% electronic health record with an **electronic signature**

### Supplier Role
- A supplier **may** provide a blank template listing the SWO elements to the treating practitioner
- A supplier **must not** fill in or complete any elements of the template

## Detailed Written Order (DSWO) — Accessories, Options & Supplies

- Required **prior to claim submission** for all separately billed options, accessories, and/or supplies
- May be **prepared by someone other than the treating practitioner**
- Must still be **reviewed and signed** by a treating practitioner before submission

### Key Flexibility — Signer Does Not Need to Match
The treating practitioner who signs the DSWO **does not need to be** the same practitioner who:
- Conducted the face-to-face encounter, or
- Wrote and signed the WOPD for the PMD base

> [!note]
> This is a **recent change**. Previously, the DSWO had to be signed by the same treating practitioner who did the face-to-face and wrote the base SWO.

### Requirements for the Practitioner Signing the DSWO
They must:
- Verify that a qualifying face-to-face encounter occurred **within six months prior** to the date of the WOPD for the base item
- Have **documentation** of that qualifying face-to-face encounter
- **Review and sign** their order

## Quick Reference

| Document | For | Who Can Prepare | Who Must Sign |
|----------|-----|-----------------|---------------|
| WOPD (SWO) | PMD base item | Treating practitioner only | Same treating practitioner |
| DSWO | Accessories, options, supplies | Anyone (including supplier staff) | Any treating practitioner |
