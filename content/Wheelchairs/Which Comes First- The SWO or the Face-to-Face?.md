## The Required Order of Events

There is no ambiguity here — the correct sequence is:

1. **Face-to-face encounter** (with ordering practitioner)
2. **LCMP wheelchair evaluation** (PT/OT) — if applicable
3. **SWO for the PMD base** (power wheelchair)
4. **Detailed SWO** (accessories) — cannot be dated prior to the base SWO

> [!important]
> The SWO and Detailed SWO **may** be dated the same day as the face-to-face/LCMP completion — they just cannot be dated **before** it.

The logic: the ordering practitioner cannot order an item before having all documentation that establishes medical necessity.

---

## Date on the SWO — Best Practice

- One of the required SWO elements is the **order date**, which has caused significant industry confusion
- **Recommended approach:** List only **one date** on both the SWO and Detailed SWO, serving as both the order date and signature date
- Instruct the ordering practitioner: *"This date is the date you sign the document"*

## Six-Month Rules

| Requirement | Timeframe | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| SWO must be written within | 6 months of face-to-face date | LCMP eval date does **not** reset this clock |
| LCMP eval must be completed within | 6 months of face-to-face date | Because SWO can't be written until LCMP is done |
| PMD delivery (all power wheelchairs & scooters) | Within 6 months of prior auth affirmation letter | Prior auth required for all PMDs |
| Manual wheelchair delivery | Within 1 year of SWO date | Standard; no ADMC |
| K0005 / E1161 delivery (if ADMC obtained) | Within 6 months of ADMC approval letter | ADMC for these is **optional** |

---

## Summary Flow

**Face-to-face encounter**
↓
**LCMP evaluation** (if applicable) — must finish within 6 months of face-to-face
↓
**SWO written for PMD base** — must be written within 6 months of face-to-face
↓
**Detailed SWO** (accessories) — same day or after base SWO
↓
**Prior authorization obtained** (required for all PMDs)
↓
**Delivery** — within 6 months of prior auth letter (PMDs) or 1 year of SWO (manual WC)
